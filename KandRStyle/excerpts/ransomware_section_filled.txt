When we computed the upper and lower bounds, we recognized that a normal distribution would not fit well for forecasting likelihood.  We took the mean of the sample from American Samoa for the lower bound (0); we took the mean from the sample from California for the upper bound (1.06); we had a separation of approximately 1 unit.  Because both means fall between 0 and 1 to the nearest whole number, we used a binary model with two options (0 and 1 events) sharing a combined likelihood of 100\%.
