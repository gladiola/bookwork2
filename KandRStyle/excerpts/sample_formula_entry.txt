When we computed the upper and lower bounds, we recognized that a normal distribution would not fit well for forecasting likelihood.  We took the mean of the sample from American Samoa for the lower bound (); we took the mean from the sample from California for the upper bound (); we had a separation of  units.  Therefore, we switched to a discrete model with all  options sharing an equal likelihood.
